What is a Form 4?

Company insiders — officers, directors, and large shareholders — must report most stock trades to the SEC within two business days on Form 4. It's the fastest public window into what people running a company are doing with their own shares.

Common transaction codes

P — Open market buy
Insider purchased shares on the open market with their own money.
S — Open market sell
Insider sold shares on the open market.
A — Award / grant
Shares granted as compensation, e.g. RSU vesting — not a cash transaction.
M — Option exercise
Insider exercised stock options, converting them into shares.
10b5-1 plan
A pre-scheduled trading plan set up in advance, often used to avoid timing questions.
